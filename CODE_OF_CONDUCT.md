# Code of conduct

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to make the thing better.

Concretely, in issues, pull requests and any other project space:

- Critique the work, not the person. "This drops a glyph when the run is rotated"
  is useful; "this is sloppy" is not.
- Assume competence and good faith, and ask before concluding otherwise. Most
  disagreements here are two people holding different facts.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or unwelcome attention of any kind. No
  demeaning comments about who someone is.
- If you are asked to stop, stop.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, and block accounts, when this is
not followed. Report a problem privately to **anton.arnaudov.pro@gmail.com**;
reports are handled discreetly and I will tell you what I did about it.

This is a short document because a long one would not change anyone's behaviour.
Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
